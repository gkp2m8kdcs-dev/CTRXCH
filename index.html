Private investment journal for global retail investors

CTRXCH is a personal investment journal that helps you track multi-currency portfolios across US, Hong Kong, China, Japan, UK, and European markets. It supports interactive candlestick, line, and area charts, complete trade journaling (buy, sell, dividends), risk analytics, sector allocation, dividend income tracking, and stock comparison — all stored privately on your device.

No account. No login. No cloud sync. No data collection. Fully offline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CTRXCH require an account or login?

No. CTRXCH works completely offline and stores all trades, watchlists, and images locally on your device. There is no account, no login, and no cloud sync.

Does CTRXCH connect to any exchange or brokerage?

No. CTRXCH is a journaling tool only. It does not connect to any exchange, brokerage, wallet, or trading platform. You record your own transactions manually.

Is CTRXCH investment or financial advice?

No. CTRXCH is a personal record-keeping and analytics tool. It does not provide investment, brokerage, tax, or legal advice. All content is for informational purposes only.

How is my data protected?

All data is stored on-device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. For full details, please read our Privacy Policy.

How can I request a data export or deletion?

Since CTRXCH does not collect data from your device, there is nothing stored on our side. All export and deletion is handled locally through the app's Settings menu. If you need assistance, contact us at karlawsokunevajob68@gmail.com.
