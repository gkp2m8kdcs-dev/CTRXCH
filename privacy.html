Privacy Policy

CTRXCH · Last updated: September 23, 2026

CTRXCH ("we", "our", "the app") is designed with privacy as a core principle. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. The short version: we do not collect any personal data from you. Everything you enter into CTRXCH stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information from CTRXCH users. Specifically:

  • No account data. CTRXCH does not require sign-up, login, email, password, or any form of user identity.
  • No usage analytics. We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel).
  • No tracking. We do not use advertising identifiers, fingerprinting, or cross-app tracking technologies.
  • No network transmission. The app does not send your trades, holdings, watchlists, notes, or images to any remote server.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

CTRXCH stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Trades you record (buy, sell, dividend), including price, quantity, fees, currency, tags, and notes.
  • Watchlists and stock metadata you add.
  • Receipt photos you attach to trades, captured via the camera or selected from your photo library.
  • App preferences such as theme, reporting currency, and chart settings.

All of this data lives in the app's local sandbox. It is never uploaded to our servers (we do not operate any), and it is never shared with third parties.

3. Permissions

CTRXCH requests the following operating-system permissions, only when you actively use the related feature:

  • Camera: Used solely to capture a receipt photo when you choose to attach one to a trade. Images are saved locally and never transmitted.
  • Photo Library: Used solely to select an existing image to attach to a trade. Images remain under your control and never leave your device.

CTRXCH does not request location, contacts, microphone, calendar, background tracking, or any other permission.

4. Children

CTRXCH is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any information from children, because we do not collect any information from anyone.

5. Third-Party Services

CTRXCH does not integrate any third-party services that process user data. All charting, analytics, persistence, and UI are implemented natively within the app.

6. Data Security

Because all data is stored on your device, security depends on the security of your device and its operating system. We recommend keeping your device locked with a passcode and your iOS version up to date. CTRXCH does not store or transmit data that could be intercepted in transit.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access & Export: All data is accessible and exportable from inside the app (Settings → Export).
  • Deletion: You can delete any trade, image, or entire dataset at any time from the app. Uninstalling CTRXCH removes all local data.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Since we do not collect data, there is nothing to withdraw. Removing the app fully erases your data from the device.

8. International Users

Because CTRXCH does not transfer any data off your device, this Privacy Policy applies equally to users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, the United States, China, Hong Kong, Japan, and any other jurisdiction.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or in applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any change. Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notice where appropriate.
